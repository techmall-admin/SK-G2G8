Road to G8

Every grade left, in order. Coins and VP spent on one grade are taken away before the next. Grades after your next one assume no research done yet.

How the numbers are worked out
  • VP = Valor Points. You can type them in millions (M) or billions (B).
  • VP needed = research levels left (Training cost + March speed) + guards left × guard cost.
  • Missing VP is covered with Ragnarök runs in the previous grade's shop. One run = all Wolf + Dragon packs (Forest packs are added when you have enough Forest coins for a full run).
  • Gold is what it costs to buy the missing projects (10 gold per project).
  • Wood, iron and stone: the same amount of each is needed to raise the Academy to the required level.
